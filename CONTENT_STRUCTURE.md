# Content Structure

The app contains 19 practical categories:

1. Coping with the Language Barrier
2. Useful Forms of Etiquette
3. Giving Information About Yourself
4. Recognizing Signs
5. Weights and Measures
6. Using Numbers
7. Dealing with Money
8. Dealing with Time
9. Locating Things
10. Describing Things and People
11. Doing Things
12. Going Places
13. Conveying Information
14. Health and Hygiene
15. Food
16. Clothing
17. Housing
18. Jobs
19. About Schools

Each category includes:

- English and Somali title
- English and Somali description
- icon key and theme key
- category-specific subcategories
- authored English expressions with natural Somali meanings
- expression examples that place the phrase in a real situation
- unique short dialogues with ordered bilingual lines
- practical question-answer pairs
- category-linked vocabulary with definitions, Somali explanations, and examples

Standalone wordlists are generated from normalized vocabulary records and vocabulary translations. English-Somali and Somali-English views are two presentations of the same records.

Signs and units of measure are stored in their own tables for focused reference screens and search.

## Authorship Policy

The reference phrasebook named in the project brief is used only for broad structural and subject-matter inspiration. The app does not reproduce source-book phrases, dialogues, wordlists, introductions, translations, or explanations. New phrasebook content must be explicitly authored; loops may insert authored collections but must not construct learning content from category names, record numbers, or generic sentence stems.

No template-generated filler is allowed. Content validation rejects numbered headwords, generic definitions, repeated expression examples, fake pronunciation, and phrases such as "for detail", "I need this word for", and artificial Somali numbering.
